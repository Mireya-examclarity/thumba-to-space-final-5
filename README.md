# Thumba to Space Final 5
My ISRO project for Stardance.
A website about India's space journey - from Thumba, Kerala (1963) to Moon, Mars and beyond. I wanted to show how India's space journey started in Thumba, Kerala, and how far we have come since then.
Being from Kilimanoor, I liked the idea of connecting a place close to home with India's space history.

### What it has


⭐ Animated Star Background – It consist of movving and twinkling stars  which can create a beautiful space atmosphere.

🚀 Personalized Rocket Launch – Enter your name and launch your own rocket from Kilimanoor(specifically).

⏳ Gaganyaan Countdown – This displays the countdown to an illustrative Gaganyaan target date.

🛰️ ISS Live Tracker – THis shows the International Space Station's live location and speed, with a simulated fallback.

🌍 NASA Picture of the Day – It will help you to discover amazing astronomy pictures using NASA's APOD API.

📺 Thumba Story Video – You can watch a video about India's inspiring space journey.

📖 Thumba History – Help you to learn how St. Mary Magdalene Church became part of India's space history in 1963.

🌙 Chandrayaan-3 – You can discover India's historic successful lunar landing on 23 August 2023.

📅 Indian Space Timeline – Help you explore important milestones in India's space exploration journey.

🛰️ Mission Control – It is a   personalized launch dashboard with a launch counter.

 Indian Space Mission Database – You can  explore Gaganyaan, Chandrayaan-3, Mangalyaan, Aditya-L1 and Aryabhata.

🪐 Interactive Solar System – You can click planets and discover interesting facts about them.

 Astronaut Quiz – This feature can test your space knowledge, also to earn points and discover your astronaut rank.

 Daily Space Discovery – Help you learn fascinating facts about planets, stars and the universe.

 Responsive Design – It works on mobile phones, tablets and computers.

 Local Launch Counter – It remembers launches in the browser using local storage.
