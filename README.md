# Thumba to Space Final 5
My ISRO project for Stardance.
A website about India's space journey - from Thumba, Kerala (1963) to Moon, Mars and beyond. I wanted to show how India's space journey started in Thumba, Kerala, and how far we have come since then.
Being from Kilimanoor, I liked the idea of connecting a place close to home with India's space history.

### What it has

⭐ Moving stars in the background to give it a space look.
🚀 A rocket launch button where you can enter your name and launch your own rocket.
⏳ A countdown for the Gaganyaan mission.
🛰️ An ISS tracker that shows the space station's location.
🌍 NASA's picture of the day.
📺 A YouTube video about India's space journey.
📖 A section about the history of Thumba and how India's space programme started.
🌙 Information about Chandrayaan-3 and its successful Moon landing.
📅 A timeline showing important events in India's space history.
👨‍🚀 A small mission control section with a launch counter.
📱 A design that works on phones and computers.
